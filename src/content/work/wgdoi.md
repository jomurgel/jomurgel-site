He didn't need a storefront. The products move through a third party, so a full catalog with a cart would've been solving a problem he doesn't have. What he needed was closer to a business card — a site that looks like the real operation, shows what he sells, and makes it easy for someone to ask for an estimate. Small on purpose, with room to grow into more when the time comes.

So that's what I built. One clean, simple site, designed around a marine red pulled straight from his service background — a nod to the reason the business runs the way it does. The color earns its keep: it sets the tone before anyone reads a word, and it keeps the whole thing from looking like every other contractor template out there.

Design and build were both mine, start to finish. Launched 8/31/2026.
